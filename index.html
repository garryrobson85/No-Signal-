📖 How to Play
1. Build Your Cast
Create or auto-generate contestants. Each has 4 stats (Physical, Social, Mental, Endurance) and an archetype that shapes how they play.
2. Set Up Teams & Rules
Assign players to tribes, pick twists, and set the merge episode. Add a Gemini AI key in General Settings for unique dialogue every episode.
3. Run Episodes
Each episode: read camp confessionals → pick a challenge → watch the race → send the losing tribe to tribal council.
4. Tribal Council
Flip each parchment in order to reveal votes. The player with the most votes is eliminated. Ties trigger a revote or fire-making challenge.
5. Post-Merge
After the merge, individual immunity challenges protect one player. Alliances, idols, and rivalries shape every vote. The last player standing wins.
6. The Finale
The final 2 or 3 face the jury — eliminated players who vote for the winner based on how the game was played.
💡 Tips: Use the ⚙ Settings to toggle haptics, particles, and scanlines. Check the Relationship Web under the Web tab to spot rivalries. The Producer panel lets you intervene in the game.
